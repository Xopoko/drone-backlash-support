Data and permissions

Privacy policy

Drone Backlash requires no registration. Game data stays on your device.

Effective September 7, 2026.
Developer: Maksim Kudriavtsev.
Contact: m.horoko@gmail.com.

In-game data

The app does not send game data, sensor readings, advertising identifiers or diagnostic records to the developer. The game has no advertising, third-party analytics or In-App Purchases.

Control settings, game progress, operation results, earned in-game currency and equipment bought with it are saved on your device. The app keeps this data in its own container until it is replaced or the app is deleted. Device backups are managed by iOS and the user; the game has no cloud account or server for restoring progress.

Sensors and permissions

Your iPhone’s relative orientation is used to control your view. Motion readings are not stored in the diagnostic log or sent to the developer. In portrait, you aim by moving your phone; touch aiming is also available in landscape.

The game does not require access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Local diagnostics

During active play, the app periodically updates a small local file, aiming for an interval of about 10 seconds. It stores up to 36 aggregate samples: time, frame rate, quality level, thermal state, elapsed game time in the current shift and the number of game effects. The app version, build number and current process identifier are also stored.

The file does not contain screen recordings, audio recordings or recordings of player actions. It is not sent automatically.

Support requests

When you email m.horoko@gmail.com, you choose what to include. The developer receives your email address, message and attachments and uses them to reply and investigate the issue you describe.

Emails are processed through Gmail and kept for as long as they are needed to handle your request. The developer does not use support requests for promotional mailings. To ask how your email is handled or request its deletion, write to the same address.

The email service is covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Support and privacy pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no forms, analytics scripts or advertising tools. The page code does not set cookies or request external fonts or other external resources when you read it.

GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. The provider may also process other technical information about requests, including browser information. See GitHub’s privacy policy for details. Following an external link opens a service with its own data-handling terms.

Questions about data

For questions about this policy or how a support request is handled, email m.horoko@gmail.com. For game instructions, visit the support page.
