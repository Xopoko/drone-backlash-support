Getting started

Choose an operation at headquarters and tap “TAKE POSITION”. Hold “FIRE” to shoot and release the button to let your weapon cool down.

To complete an operation, shoot down the required number of targets and hold your position until the end of the 90-second shift. One star unlocks the next operation.

The interface follows your iPhone’s language settings.

Controls

Portrait: aim by rotating your iPhone. The fire button is at the bottom center. Touch aiming is not available in this orientation.

Landscape: aim by rotating your phone, or enable “Touch aiming” in settings. You can also choose the side for the fire button and adjust sensitivity, sound and haptic feedback. Your previous control settings are kept when you return to landscape.

At the start of an operation and after a pause, the game calibrates the controls to your phone’s current position. If motion sensors are unavailable, turn your iPhone to landscape to use touch aiming.

Progress and purchases

Each drone you shoot down earns 200 units of in-game currency. Currency is earned by playing; it cannot be bought, transferred or exchanged for real money. Ammunition is free. The shop offers permanent upgrades, weapons and autonomous support. The app has no real-money payments or In-App Purchases.

Settings, your balance, equipment and operation results are saved on your device. The game has no cloud account for restoring progress. Deleting the app removes its local saves; device backups are managed by iOS and the user.

If something goes wrong

In your email, describe the problem and the steps that lead to it. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version and screen orientation. Attach a screenshot of the game if it helps explain the problem.

For sound problems, check the in-game “Sound” setting and mention whether you are using speakers or headphones. If the game becomes less smooth, tell us how many minutes pass before it starts happening.
